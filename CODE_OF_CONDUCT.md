# Code of Conduct

Web3tribe University serves learners, instructors and communities, including people who are new
to technology and new to the internet. Everyone taking part in this project — in issues, pull
requests, discussions, translations and course content — is expected to make it a respectful,
welcoming place. This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our standard

Expected behaviour:
- Be respectful, patient and constructive, especially with newcomers and non-native English speakers.
- Give and accept feedback graciously; focus on the work, not the person.
- Respect people's privacy. Never share personal data about learners or contributors.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.

Unacceptable behaviour:
- Harassment, insults, discrimination or demeaning comments of any kind, including on the basis of
  language, nationality, ethnicity, religion, gender, disability, age or background.
- Sexualised language or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing others' private information, or attacking the platform's users or infrastructure.
- Any conduct a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and may warn, temporarily restrict or permanently
ban anyone who breaks this code. Report a problem privately to **dinfadashe@gmail.com** with the
subject "Code of Conduct". Reports are handled confidentially and in good faith, and the person
reporting will not face retaliation.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces and when someone represents the project publicly.
